Wait, you still haven't been to the amazing rooftop garden in Cambridge?

A unique venue in the heart of Kendall invites you to socialize.

August 13, 2026·7pm - 9pm·Urban Park roof garden (Cambridge)·Salome Püntener

Have you already recovered after all that partying while celebrating the First of August? For our August getTogETHer, we’ll again gather at the unique Urban Park roof garden in Kendall.

For a good cause

Lamplighter Brewing is offering the chance to donate $5 for a charity in exchange for one of their excellent brews. This month’s charity, Rosie’s Place, offers a multitude of services and programs to support women struggling with homelessness and poverty. Their organization provides food, sanctuary, legal aid, and more to thousands of women each year.

Logistics

Complimentary hot dogs and pretzels as well as NA options are provided; they tend to run out quickly, so be sure to come early (they start distributing at 4:30pm)! There is also a free Pickleball court on-site with paddles provided for those who still have a bit too much energy left in the day 😉 Please RSVP to find out with whom you could play!

Looking forward to a great evening out!
