About

Bringing brilliant people togETHer.

The ETH Alumni New England Chapter (NEC) connects ETH Zurich graduates and former researchers throughout the US Northeast. Operating out of the Boston/Cambridge biotech and tech hub, the chapter organizes networking events, tech talks, and socials in collaboration with groups like Swissnex in Boston.

Founded in October 2010, the New England Chapter serves to foster business contacts, share advancements in science and technology, and help expats integrate into the local innovation ecosystem.

Who we are

ETH alumni are those who have either studied or worked at the Swiss Federal Institute of Technology in Zurich (ETH Zurich) for at least two semesters (one academic year).

You’ll be connected through activities that promote sharing our common passion for science, technology, and architecture. As a knowledge bridge between the USA and ETH Zurich, our website serves double-duty, keeping you informed of the latest breakthroughs of the Institute and helping you to create friendships and valuable business contacts with other alumni. Our future is your future!

How to join

Members of the New England Chapter are automatically members of ETH Alumni. While it is optional, you can officially register for membership.

By registering, you can choose the activities of interest and benefit to you. Everyone’s requests will be accommodated so that the NEC will be a chapter totally suited to your needs. Let’s get connected!
